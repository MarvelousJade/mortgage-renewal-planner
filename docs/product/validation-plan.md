# Product validation plan

## Audience

Canadian homeowners with a mortgage renewal in the next 12 months. Mortgage brokers are a secondary audience who may use the tool to explain scenarios.

## Riskiest assumptions

1. People receive multiple offers that are difficult to compare.
2. Remaining balance and term-end balance matter in addition to the advertised payment.
3. Visitors will trust an independent calculator when its methodology is explicit.

## Initial interviews

Interview at least ten homeowners and two licensed mortgage professionals. Do not collect account numbers, documents or other sensitive data. Ask participants to use fictional or rounded figures during moderated tests.

## MVP success measures

- 20 completed usability sessions
- 50 completed offer comparisons
- At least 10 returning visitors
- Three users willing to provide a public testimonial
- Fewer than 10% of calculator submissions fail validation

These are validation targets, not current achievements.

## Deferred features

- Saved scenarios and authentication
- Renewal-date email reminders
- Bank of Canada rate context
- PDF exports
- Mortgage-servicing request workflow
- Legacy SOAP/XML adapter demonstration
